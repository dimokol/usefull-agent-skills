# ✍️ AI writing tone

Make agent-written text read human, plus a product-copy voice guide.
`plain-human-tone.md` is the canonical guide; `product-copy-voice.md` is a worked example
of adapting it to a branded product voice.

| Item | What it solves | Portability |
|---|---|---|
| [plain-human-tone](plain-human-tone.md) | Make the agent's own writing (PRs, commits, chat, docs) read human: no em-dashes, no "not X but Y", drop AI-tell vocabulary. The blocklist + techniques + ship checklist. | 🟢 |
| [product-copy-voice](product-copy-voice.md) | How to derive a clear, laconic, human product-copy voice on top of the base rules, for non-technical and elderly users. | 🟢 |
